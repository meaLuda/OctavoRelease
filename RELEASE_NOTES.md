Octavo Reader 1.0.4: PDF reading rebuilt for phones and tablets, and read-aloud for PDFs.

- PDF layout (Aa > Layout): Fit (Auto, Width, Page), Crop margins (on by default on phones), Scroll (Vertical or
  Paged sideways page turns), Direction (Left to right or Right to left). Remembered per book.
- Pinch zoom and double-tap to zoom into a column; sharp after zooming, no drift; kept across pages and reopening.
- Page slider with thumbnails; tap the middle of the page to show or hide the controls.
- Read aloud for PDFs: follows columns, skips headers, footers and page numbers, highlights the sentence being
  read, turns pages for you; mini player with speed and sleep timer; natural voices with Octavo Cloud.
- Mobile: the library opens on first launch with a pointer to the ribbon menu; desktop-only actions hidden on
  phones; new "Copy mobile diagnostics" command for bug reports.
- Copying PDF text joins wrapped lines and keeps paragraphs.
