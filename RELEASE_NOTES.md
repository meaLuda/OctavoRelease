Beta for testers (BRAT). Not shipped to directory users.

PDF reading, rebuilt for phones and tablets:
- Layout panel (Aa): Fit (Auto, Width, Page), Crop margins (on by default on phones), Scroll (Vertical or
  Paged), Direction (Left to right or Right to left). Remembered per book.
- Pinch zoom and double-tap to zoom into a column; sharp after zooming, no drift; kept across pages,
  rotation and reopening.
- Paged mode: swipe or tap the edges to turn pages sideways like a book.
- Page slider with a thumbnail grid; tap the middle of the page to show or hide the controls.
- Copying text joins wrapped lines and keeps paragraphs.

Read aloud for PDFs:
- Follows the reading order (columns, skips headers, footers and page numbers), highlights the sentence
  being read and turns pages for you.
- Mini player: previous and next sentence, speed, sleep timer; "Speak" on a selection reads from there.
- Natural voices with Octavo Cloud, system voices otherwise.
