Octavo Reader 1.1.3

iPhone and iPad
- PDF highlight, note and other selection menu buttons work again (the tap used to clear the selection first).
- EPUB books are no longer pushed down the screen by a blank band.

Reading
- The screen sleeps again after you stop reading or close the book.
- With Obsidian Sync, reading time and finished books from each device are kept instead of one device overwriting the other.
- Read aloud keeps its word highlight and ruler in later chapters, and cannot start twice.
- Reading past midnight counts on each day.

Highlights and notes
- A label or quote with a line break no longer hides the highlight or duplicates it.
- Quotes are matched more reliably (soft hyphens, ligatures, paragraphs), and PDF quote text matches the page.
- Imported highlights go to the right book only (Dune no longer takes Dune Messiah's highlights), and highlights without a date no longer show 1970.
- Literature templates with quoted placeholders stay valid, and dates use your own time zone.

Define, cards and stats
- Define without AI finds capitalised words and words next to punctuation, and skips empty definitions.
- Quote cards wrap long words and Chinese or Japanese text and never run over the title.
- Year in review keeps what you wrote in the note and counts only this year's highlights.
- Imported finished books count for the year they were read.

PDF
- Region embeds and previews of Octavo Cloud books render.
- Area links on scanned pages scroll to the right place, and links Octavo cannot use say so and keep your place.
- Two page spreads and Text view reach the last page, and Text view keeps roman numeral headings.

Library and more
- Collection and status actions on unread books no longer mark them as being read, and errors are shown.
- Add books lists small PDFs and says what it skipped.
- Each AI provider keeps its own key, model and address, and AI requests time out instead of hanging.
- Changing the default theme updates books that are already open.
- Copy and Save to vocabulary say when they fail.
