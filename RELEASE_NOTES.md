Octavo Reader 1.1.2

Fixes
- Reading time counts only time spent reading: a book left open in a tab is no longer logged as hours of reading.
- Reading streaks are right across the October clock change (today was not counted, and one day was counted twice).
- "Finished in" counts every way a book is finished (end of a PDF, the menus, imports), once.
- Progress is never saved as "not a number" for EPUBs that report no section sizes, so those books no longer show 0%.
- A page turned just before opening another book in the same tab is no longer saved into the other book's note.
- Ask answers and chapter summaries stay in their own sections of the book note.
- The Reading line in your daily note never lands above its properties.
- Repairing duplicate notes keeps a PDF's page, and no longer joins two different books.
- One page PDFs read 100% once open.
- Octavo Cloud: moving a book that is already in your cloud library works; after OCR, the searchable copy replaces the old scan on the device that asked for it; read aloud stops waiting when a request is cancelled.
